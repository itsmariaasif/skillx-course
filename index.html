Chapter 2 · Prompt Engineering

Prompt Engineering Journey — Studio Setup

Is chapter mein prompt ka asal farq dekha gaya: simple prompt vs engineered prompt — aik hi idea, do mukhtalif nateeje.

Simple prompt (pehle diya gaya)

“Generate a professional studio photography, this product.”

Nateeja: aik aam si tasveer mili. Phir prompt engineering se prompts dobara likhe gaye — tafseel ke saath.

Engineered prompt (baad wala)

“Generate a luxury futuristic video-editing studio centered around a sleek black editing workstation with dual high-resolution monitors. Screens display cinematic footage, advanced video timelines, AI-generated imagery, motion graphics, VFX compositing and color grading. Surround the workstation with subtle floating holographic timeline elements, AI visual icons, geometric motion-graphic shapes, data particles and purple cinematic light streaks. Camera angle: 3/4 front perspective, slightly above desk level, 35mm cinematic lens, deep focus, 8K resolution.”

Doosra prompt — ultra-premium version — mein hero camera angle, 40mm lens aur realistic proportions tak likhe gaye. Nateeja: pro-level studio wali pictures.

Learning: Prompt jitna detailed hoga, result utna hi premium hoga. Cheez, angle, lens, lighting aur style — sab likho.
Chapter 3 · AI Video

AI Video Editor — Copy. Paste. Prompt. Create.

Video editing ka purana tareeqa bhool jao — timeline, keyframes aur rendering ka jhanjhat khatam. Is system mein bas 4 steps: COPY karo, PASTE karo, PROMPT do, CREATE karo. Bina camera, bina studio ke sirf apne lafzon se professional videos banti hain.

4 pillars — har aik samjho

  • 1. Fast Creation — minutes mein video ready. Pehle 30 second ki cinematic video mein 4–5 ghante lagte thay. Ab prompt likho — “A cinematic shot of snowy mountains at sunset, drone view, 4K” — aur AI 2–3 minute mein video bana deta hai. Reels, ads, YouTube intros aur backgrounds — sab 5 minute mein. Din mein 10–20 videos mumkin.
  • 2. AI Powered — AI apka assistant hai. Aap director ho, AI apka cameraman + editor hai. Aap idea dete ho, AI khud camera angle, light aur movement tay karta hai. Tools: Runway, Pika, Luma Dream Machine, Sora.
  • 3. High Quality Output — 4K Ultra HD studio quality. AI videos ab cartoon nahi lagteen — bilkul real lagti hain. Cinematic camera movements, drone shot, close-up, slow-motion, perfect lighting aur colors — client ko impress karne wali quality.
  • 4. Creative Freedom — jaisa socho, waisa banao. Aapki soch hi apki limit hai: cinematic mountains, futuristic city, product ad, cartoon story — koi location, koi time, koi style, sab possible. Mehange cameras, locations ya bari team ki zaroorat nahi.

Ye course do hisson mein hai: AI Video Editor (tool) + Learn AI Video Creation (skill). Socho — jo idea zehan mein hai, wohi cinematic video ban jaye. Ab ye khwab nahi, skill hai.

Benefit: Video dekh kar koi nahi kahega ke ye AI se bani hai — aur yehi asal kamyabi hai.
Chapter 4 · Thumbnails

Master Prompt Thumbnail Class

Is class mein thumbnails ka tajziya (analysis) kiya gaya — ke aik thumbnail click kyun hota hai aur doosra nazar andaaz kyun ho jata hai.

Class ka focus

  • Saaf subject: thumbnail mein aik wazeh markazi cheez ho — nazar pehli jhalak mein samajh aaye.

Chapter 5 · AI Movies

How to Create a Movie — AI Movie Workflow

AI se mukammal movie banane ka step-by-step tareeqa — idea se le kar tasveeron aur voiceover tak:

  1. Idea prompt: AI video creation ke liye aik creative concept share karo — jese Hollywood style mein dikhana ke AI video kaise kaam karti hai.
  2. Concept polish: concept ko mazeed behtar aur tafseel se likho — “polish this concept, the more enhancement.”
  3. Scene-by-scene script: concept ko scene dar scene raw script aur video prompts mein badlo.

  5. Character reference: apni character sheet attach karo taake chehre ki pehchan har scene mein mehfooz rahe, aur apna logo bhi saath rakho. Pehli reference image 9:16 portrait ratio mein banao, phir agle scenes ki images usi reference se banao.

Chapter 6 · YouTube Automation

YouTube Automation — Foundations

YouTube automation ka bunyadi dhancha: topic selection, scripting, voiceover, editing aur thumbnail — aur phir aik aham sawal: saturation vs competition.

Saturation vs competition

  • Competition: aik hi niche ko mukhtalif nazariye (perspective) se banana — content ki demand zyada hoti hai, bara audience milta hai.
  • Saturation: aik hi cheez ki 100 copies — sab se zyada khatarnaak yahi hai. Zero learning, zero creativity.

Policies — mass demonetization se bacho

  • IP, recovery, emails aur AdSense ka khayal rakho.
  • AdSense ke nuqsan: aarzi lalach ke peeche 1–2 mahine mein jitna nikala, us se zyada nuqsan ye ke AdSense kharab ho jaye — phir market se khareeda hua ya kisi aur ki maloomat par apply karna bhi mushkil ho jata hai.

Channel levels

  • Level 1 — AI based: mukammal AI par mabni channel.
  • Level 2 — Classical + AI + visual stock + videos: mila jula tareeqa.
  • Level 3 — Highly experienced: topics, scripting, visuals + AI — sab par maharat.

Random vs specific workflow

Random: koi idea nahi ke content kya banana hai — bilkul blank. Specific: topic pehle chuno (jese wedding card), phir uski script nikalo, phir poori video banao. Channel analysis ke liye social media tools aur keywords istemal karo.

Content pillars

Content pillars 4–5 hone chahiye — misaal ke taur par “disturbing moments” ke pillars: movies, reality TV shows, documentary + horror, aur cartoon. Pillars na banana nuqsan hai — channel ka rukh hi wazeh nahi hota.

Chapter 7 · Scriptwriting

Scriptwriting — The 5-Step Formula

Har achhi script aur har clickable title ke peeche aik hi formula hai — 5 steps:

1Subject
2Situation
3Triggers
4Tension
5Promise
  • Subject: woh cheez jis par aap discuss karne ja rahe ho.
  • Situation: woh pehlu jo aap discuss karna chahte ho.
  • Triggers: jo apke title ko zyada clickable banate hain.
  • Tension: woh baat jo dekhne wale ke zehan mein aaye aur woh click kare.
  • Promise: woh jo aap ne video mein deliver karna hai.

Worked example — disturbing movie scenes

Subject: disturbing movie scenes. Situation: what scene? what movies? how disturbing? what is inside them? Tension: scenes — movie disturbing. Promise: disturbing, sleepless, restless, chills. Triggers: horror movies, disturbing scenes.

Title: “Horror movies disturbing scenes that can make you sleepless.” — Sleepless / restless / terrifying / chilling — yehi alfaz click dilate hain.

Title research ka tareeqa

AI search tools (jaise Gemini) se aik angle par mukhtalif titles banao — misaal: aik watch brand ke “downfall” angle par 5-step formula se mukhtalif titles. Phir 5-point breakdown likho: subject kya hai, situation kya hai, triggers konsay hain, tension kahan hai, aur promise kya deliver hoga.

Scriptwriting ka usool: pehle 5 steps kagaz par, phir script. Title formula se banta hai, andaze se nahi.
Chapter 8 · Prompt Practice

Prompt Engineering — Practice Work

Is practice work mein identity-preserving prompts ki mashq ki gayi — apni uploaded photo ko reference bana kar, chehre ki pehchan bilkul mehfooz rakhte hue, bilkul natural tasaveer banana.

Prompt 1 — natural selfie technique

“Use my uploaded photo as the primary identity reference and preserve my facial identity accurately. Create a completely natural, unposed selfie of me standing next to another person in the United States. The photo should look like a real phone selfie taken spontaneously — not a professional photoshoot. Both casually dressed, relaxed, naturally looking toward the phone camera. Realistic facial expressions, natural skin texture, subtle imperfections, authentic hair details, ordinary everyday lighting.”

Prompt 2 — candid photo technique

“Use the two attached reference images as identity references for the two people. Preserve their individual facial identity, facial structure, skin tone, hairstyle and overall appearance accurately. Create a completely natural-looking candid photograph of both people together, as if one of them casually took the photo on a phone. Keep the appearance realistic and unretouched, with natural skin texture, subtle imperfections, realistic hair strands and authentic facial expressions. Aspect ratio: 4:3.”

Seekhi hui baatein

  • Identity reference wazeh likho: facial identity, structure, skin tone, hairstyle — sab mehfooz rakhna hai.
  • “Natural, unposed, candid” likhne se tasveer photoshoot jaisi nahi, asal zindagi jaisi lagti hai.
  • Lighting, skin texture aur chhoti imperfections ka zikr realism badhata hai.
  • Airport, street ya roz-marra ki jagah ka zikr background ko yaqeen-dahani deta hai.
Chapter 9 · YouTube Roadmap

YouTube Automation Roadmap — Podcast-Style Channel Blueprint

Aik kamyab beginner English-learning channel ka mukammal blueprint — competitor ka tajziya, script structure, visual design, AI production pipeline aur technical setup — taake isi tarz ki videos khud banayi ja saken.

Competitor breakdown — ye tarz kyun kaam karta hai

  • Target audience: absolute beginners (A1–A2) jo native-speed English se ghabrate hain — unhein slow, saaf, dohraai wali guftagu chahiye.
  • Format: “podcast” style — do hosts ki scripted guftagu, seekhne ke liye banayi gayi.
  • Visuals: 2D flat vector cartoons — rang-birange, dostana aur live-action se saste.
  • Thumbnails: bare alfaz (“My Daily Routine”, “Tell Me About Yourself”) jo mobile screen par chha jate hain.
  • Hook: aik khaas masla hal karna — jese “apne din ke bare mein kaise baat karein” — aur sahi vocabulary aur jumle dena.

Production pipeline — 4 steps

  • Step A — Script Generation (LLM): ChatGPT ya Claude se 5-minute podcast script — do hosts, aik topic, simple tense, vocabulary section, slow dialogue aur “common mistakes” section; jumle 10 alfaz se chhote.
  • Step B — Voice Generation (TTS): do mukhtalif awaazein (female + male), stability aur similarity high. Insani lehje ke liye “uhm” aur “ah” fillers add karo. Free alternative: Edge-TTS — muft hai lekin zyada robotic lagta hai.
  • Step C — Visual Generation: Midjourney, DALL-E 3 ya Gemini image generation se 2D flat vector illustration — podcast desk par do log, warm colors, simple background. Thumbnail par Canva ya Photoshop se bara bold text lagao.
  • Step D — Video Assembly (FFmpeg): static image + audio track ko script se joro, halki “pulse” effect do taake tasveer bilkul static na lage. Whisper se subtitles (.ass captions) banao — beginner channels ke liye ye lazmi hai.

Automation: manually edit mat karo — Python pipeline se script, voice, image aur assembly ko aik nizam mein joro. Aik aur channel idea bhi class mein aaya: family channel — do chhote bachay, mum aur dad — roz-marra ki kahani aur seekhne wali videos ke saath.

Chapter 10 · Airdrops

Airdrop Notes — Crypto Airdrops, Earn From Mobile

Mobile se earning — laptop ki zaroorat nahi. Airdrop kya hai? Aik crypto project ki taraf se apke wallet mein bheje gaye muft coins — aap chhote tasks karte ho, project apko tokens se reward deta hai.

Airdrop kaise kaam karta hai

  1. Join: app download karo ya project join karo.
  2. Tasks: daily check-in, follow, test, invite — tasks mukammal karo.
  3. Points: active rehne se points jama hote hain.
  4. Coins: project coins apke wallet mein bhejta hai.

Projects muft coins kyun dete hain?

  • Users hasil karna — naye projects ko log chahiye jo unhein aazmayein.
  • Community banana — early members wafadar support bante hain.
  • Coins phelana — coins chand haathon mein nahi, bohat se haathon tak pahunchein.

Mobile se kamane ke 2 tareeqe

  • Free coin mining: trusted app kholo, daily tap ya check-in karo, waqt ke saath coins jama karo.
  • Airdrop tasks: project ke chhote tasks mukammal karo aur airdrop ke liye qualify karo.

Trust rules — hamesha yaad rakho

Sirf trusted apps istemal karo. Apna seed phrase kabhi kisi ko mat do aur claim karne ke liye kabhi paise mat do.

Misaal projects: Pi, Notcoin, Hamster Kombat, Uniswap — mobile mining, Telegram tap games aur early-user rewards. Kuch airdrops 2024 mein mile (Uni airdrop 2020 mein mila tha). Har airdrop pay nahi karta — kabhi achha reward milta hai, kabhi kuch nahi; kuch projects kabhi launch hi nahi hote. Waqt: chhote — dinon se hafton; bare — maheenon se saalon.

Referral codes: har app apko apna code deti hai — dost apke code se join karein to dono ko bonus coins milte hain. Agla hissa: advanced airdrop — mazeed gehri tafseel ke saath.
